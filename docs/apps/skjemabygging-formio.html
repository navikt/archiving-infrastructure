Forms and user journeys

Bygger and FyllUt

One monorepo contains the internal form-building tools and the citizen-facing form runtime, along with shared packages and backend services.

Bygger defines the form. FyllUt renders it. Keeping both applications in one monorepo lets them share form models and components, but they have different audiences, authentication, and release paths.

What it does

  • Bygger lets NAV employees create, edit, migrate, translate, preview, and publish form.io form definitions.
  • FyllUt renders published forms for citizens, supports logged-in and unauthenticated use, and coordinates digital or paper submission.
  • Shared packages hold common domain models, backend utilities, and frontend components.
  • Bygger backend publishes selected form content to skjemautfylling-formio through the GitHub API.

Role in the flow

Bygger reads and writes form data in forms-api. Publishing creates a deployable content change in skjemautfylling-formio. FyllUt later renders that content, asks innsending-api to store the application and create a PDF, and sends attachment-heavy digital submissions to SendInn.

What it does not do

It does not own permanent application storage or archive documents in Joark. It also does not hold production's published form content as the final deployment artifact. skjemautfylling-formio fills that role.
